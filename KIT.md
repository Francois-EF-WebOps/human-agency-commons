# KIT.md — Local Needs-to-Pilot Kit

**Purpose:** design and evaluate one small, locally grounded intervention without assuming that a plausible idea works.

## Read first
- `README.md`
- existing pilot brief, evidence policy, consent/privacy guidance and templates
- relevant Cabo Verde/local-source notes before selecting an intervention

## Requirements pack
Record: community/user need, who defined it, who may be excluded, baseline, desired outcome, consent and refusal path, foreseeable harms, data minimisation, responsible owner, duration, budget ceiling, exit conditions and evaluation method.

## Procurement pack
Start with no-cost and locally available resources. For purchased items record specification, quantity, local supplier/source, dated price, transport, maintenance, alternatives, accessibility, and total cost. Separate quotations from estimates. Do not collect personal data or run an intervention without a lawful, ethical and consent-based basis.

## Procedure
1. Choose one bounded need, not a broad social problem.
2. Verify local context using relevant primary/local sources and direct consent-based input where appropriate.
3. Define a small test with baseline and measurable outcome.
4. Identify risks, unintended effects, opt-out, safeguarding and privacy controls.
5. Build a BOM and operational checklist if physical materials are needed.
6. Run only after prerequisites and permissions are met.
7. Record anonymised aggregate results, adverse events, limitations and participant feedback.
8. Decide to stop, revise or repeat based on evidence.

## Acceptance
- [ ] Need and intended beneficiaries are explicit.
- [ ] Consent, refusal and exit are practical.
- [ ] Risks and privacy are reviewed before data collection.
- [ ] Costs and local sourcing are evidenced.
- [ ] Baseline and outcome measures are defined.
- [ ] Results include negative or null outcomes.
- [ ] No unsupported causal claim is made.

Do not label a pilot successful based on participation, a story or a completed checklist alone; define outcome evidence first.
