# Governance

## Current stage

Human Agency Commons is an early-stage, maintainer-coordinated public project. Public visibility does not by itself mean that all decisions are community-approved or that the project represents Cabo Verdean communities.

## Decision principles

- Explain substantive changes and their rationale.
- Prefer evidence, transparency, and reversible decisions.
- Disclose conflicts of interest that could materially affect a contribution.
- Invite affected-community input before presenting a proposal as locally legitimate.
- Preserve uncertainty and documented disagreement rather than silently erasing it.
- Separate editorial review from claims of scientific, professional, or community endorsement.

## Roles

**Contributors** provide sources, analysis, translations, critiques, and proposals.

**Reviewers** check scope, evidence traceability, clarity, privacy, attribution, and foreseeable harm.

**Maintainers** manage repository structure, resolve process issues, and make provisional editorial decisions. Maintainers should explain consequential decisions and welcome good-faith corrections.

## Evidence status

A source being listed does not mean it has been independently validated. A claim being accepted for tracking does not mean it is proven. An intervention described in this repository must be labelled according to the strength and relevance of its evidence.

## Changes and accountability

Use issues for questions or proposals and pull requests for concrete changes. For disputed or high-impact claims, record the disagreement, evidence on each side, uncertainty, and what would change the assessment. Do not claim formal community consultation unless it occurred and can be described accurately and safely.

## Future governance

As participation grows, review how maintainership, moderation, language inclusion, conflicts of interest, community representation, and appeals should work. Any move toward shared governance should be explicit and documented rather than implied.
