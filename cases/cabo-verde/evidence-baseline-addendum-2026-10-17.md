# Evidence baseline addendum — water, healthcare and housing

**Added:** 2026-10-17  
**Scope:** Targeted follow-up to the first Cabo Verde evidence baseline. This is still desk research, not a comprehensive national assessment.

## Water: primary INE source located

The INE report *Estatísticas das Condições de Vida dos Agregados Familiares, 2024* contains a drinking-water table with urban/rural breakdowns. Its published extract reports that 46.5% of households drank untreated water and 27.2% used bottled water. These categories must be interpreted using the survey's definitions. The figure for untreated water is a reported household practice; it does not prove that all such water was contaminated or that the survey directly tested water quality.

**Next verification:** record the exact question, denominator, table, category definitions and sampling notes from the full report.

Source: [INE — Estatísticas das Condições de Vida dos Agregados Familiares, 2024](https://ine.cv/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/imc-2024-condicoes-de-vida-ultimo.pdf).

## Healthcare: workforce distribution is a strategic concern; figures are historical

WHO's Cabo Verde Country Cooperation Strategy 2024–2028 identifies health workforce availability/distribution and quality of care as priorities. It describes equitable distribution across inhabited islands as a challenge and reports historical workforce-density figures. This supports investigating access and distribution; it does not establish current staffing, waiting times, or the condition of every facility.

**Next verification:** extract the strategy's reference years and compare with current Ministry of Health staffing, facility, waiting-time and referral data, including island-level reporting.

Source: [WHO — Country Cooperation Strategy 2024–2028: Cabo Verde](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789290313984).

## Housing: separate stakeholder assessment from official announcements and outcomes

In October 2025, Inforpress reported that the National Association of Cape Verdean Municipalities described a significant housing deficit in quantity and quality and cited municipal financial constraints. This is a stakeholder statement, not a quantified census or independent estimate.

In April 2025, the government announced a proposed rent-to-own programme and reported around 300 units under construction across Praia, São Vicente and Sal at that time. This establishes what was announced, not whether the units were completed, allocated, affordable, or sufficient to reduce housing need.

**Next verification:** compare the stakeholder statement with INE census/housing-stock data; find programme legislation, construction and completion updates, allocations, affordability rules and beneficiary outcomes.

Sources: [Inforpress — municipalities and housing deficit](https://www.inforpress.cv/en/article-5494); [Government of Cabo Verde — rent-to-own housing announcement](https://www.governo.cv/governo-vai-avancar-com-implementacao-de-programas-de-renda-resolvel-para-facilitar-acesso-a-habitacao/).
