# Cabo Verde pilot

## Purpose

Use Cabo Verde as an initial applied case for evidence-led, locally informed work on human agency, everyday wellbeing, and community resilience. This is a pilot scope, not a claim to represent Cabo Verdean people or institutions.

## Important boundary

The challenge areas below are provisional research questions. They require verification by island, municipality, population, timeframe, and source. Do not present this list as a validated ranking of national problems.

## Initial research areas

1. Cost of living, income insecurity, unemployment, and underemployment
2. Inter-island transport and access to work and services
3. Water, sanitation, drought, and climate resilience
4. Healthcare access, staffing, and retention
5. Affordable housing
6. Education-to-work transitions
7. Gender inequality and unpaid care
8. Food security, affordability, and import dependence
9. Waste and energy systems
10. Press, television, radio, digital content, audio, and influence

## Research protocol

For each proposed claim, identify its geographic scope and timeframe, locate traceable evidence, record local reporting and language where available, seek contrary evidence, and state what the source does not establish. Avoid extrapolating from Santiago to every island or treating a national average as every household's experience.

## Intervention protocol

Before recommending a solution, map existing services and community initiatives, identify who experiences the barrier, estimate practical costs and maintenance, assess who might be excluded, and define consent, privacy, evaluation, and stop criteria.

## Religion and other forms of influence

Do not categorise religion itself as addiction. Investigate specific conduct and consequences—such as coercion, financial exploitation, isolation, interference with healthcare, or loss of meaningful choice—only where evidence supports the concern. Apply consistent standards to all relevant institutions and platforms.

## Current status

No challenge area in this file should be read as independently verified or ranked. Populate the evidence and claim ledgers before making stronger claims.
