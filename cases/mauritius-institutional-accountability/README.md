# Casebook: Institutional Accountability, Complaint Response, and Evidence

- Status: research draft; selected public sources identified, not a completed jurisdiction-wide review.
- Prepared: 2026-10-24.
- Role in Human Agency Commons: comparative methods case. Cabo Verde remains the first applied pilot; Mauritius is a comparator, not a substitute for local Cabo Verde evidence.

## Research question

What can public records establish about the gap between institutional policy, actual response, independent scrutiny, and practical remedy—and what cannot safely be inferred?

## 1. Policy and reported response: Mauritius scouting, 2025

Local reporting describes a complaint reportedly made to the Mauritius Scouts Association safety commission in February 2025, later police reporting, and suspensions after public coverage in August. A 2023 Safe From Harm policy is a relevant baseline document.

Use: compare the written policy with the public chronology and identify unanswered procedural questions.

Do not infer: guilt, intentional concealment, motive, or that publicity caused a later action solely because events occurred in sequence.

## 2. Statutory investigation: OCO / XYO

The official 2018–2019 Ombudsperson for Children annual report includes an XYO investigation chapter concerning residential care institutions, response to concerns, and recommendations.

Use: extract findings and recommendation follow-up with page-level references.

Do not infer: each person's motive or treat an institutional report as a substitute for all underlying records.

Source: https://oco.govmu.org/Documents/WEB_FINAL_Annual_Report_2018-19.pdf

## 3. Administrative remedy: Ombudsman annual report 2024

The Mauritius Ombudsman's 2024 annual report records case handling and interventions.

Use: distinguish the existence of a complaint route from timeliness, enforceability, and case-specific results. Capture remedies and unresolved recommendations.

Do not infer: the overall success rate from a few examples.

Sources:
- https://www.theioi.org/downloads/ab9an/Ombudsman%20Mauritius%20-%20Annual%20Report%20-%20EN.pdf
- https://www.theioi.org/ioi-news/current-news/launching-of-ombudsman-s-annual-report-2024

## 4. Digital artefacts: a specific ERT determination

A 2024 Employment Relations Tribunal determination describes examination of a laptop and retrieval of relevant emails in one employment dispute.

Use: read the full decision and record how the tribunal describes the artefacts, parties' positions, disputed issues, reasoning, and disposition.

Do not infer: a universal rule of admissibility, completeness, authorship, or truth from a single decision.

Source: https://ert.govmu.org/Documents/Awards/Employment%20Relations%20Tribunal/List%20of%20Awards%20year%202024/July/determinationhurrydossvbeijingrn58-24.pdf

## Claim-level evidence discipline

Every entry should include narrow claim wording; evidence class; exact source URL and passage/page when available; whether the source was fully opened, partially inspected, or found only through a search result; what it does not establish; corroboration and source dependence; counterevidence; privacy, consent, safety, copyright and jurisdiction notes; and the next verification step.

Do not equate repeated publication of one statement with independent corroboration. Do not treat a missing record as proof of deliberate deletion. Do not generalise from one island or institution to another without identifying comparable mechanisms and conditions.

## Practical output: minimum viable record preservation

Maintain a neutral chronology, preserve original records through authorised tools, keep secure copies, record collection method and time zone, and separate observation from interpretation. Hashes can detect later file changes but do not establish authorship or truth. Do not automatically change device state; the appropriate action depends on the incident and risk. Avoid unauthorised access and public disclosure of sensitive third-party information.

## Transferability limits

This casebook is about method and evidence classification. It does not establish that Mauritius's institutions, remedies, or complaint pathways exist in Cabo Verde. The Cabo Verde pilot must use local law, local-language sources, community perspectives, and its own evidence ledger.
