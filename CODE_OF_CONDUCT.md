# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Human Agency Commons aims to be a respectful, evidence-led space for people with different experiences, identities, beliefs, and levels of expertise. We focus on claims, conduct, and consequences rather than demeaning people or assigning collective blame.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and specific.
- Distinguish criticism of an idea, institution, or practice from attacks on people.
- Welcome corrections and disclose uncertainty.
- Respect consent, privacy, copyright, and cultural context.
- Do not fabricate evidence or misrepresent another contributor.
- Avoid harassment, threats, slurs, intimidation, and unwanted exposure of personal information.
- Do not pressure contributors to disclose personal experiences, beliefs, health information, or identity.

## Evidence and disagreement

Strong criticism is welcome when it is relevant and grounded. Beliefs or affiliations alone do not establish harmful conduct. Evaluate observable actions and outcomes using consistent standards, regardless of whether the subject is a company, public body, religious group, political movement, employer, influencer, or technology provider.

## Response

Project maintainers may edit, decline, or remove contributions that violate these standards, especially where privacy, safety, or fabricated evidence is involved. A person affected by a moderation decision may request a review through the repository's issue process without publishing sensitive details.

This initial code is a working policy and may be improved through contributor feedback.
