## Guardrails

- A remembered experience deserves respectful consideration, but it does not by itself prove every detail or inferred cause.
- Separate observable behaviour and its impact from claims about hidden motives or coordination.
- Do not spend more time collecting information than the decision is worth.
- Prefer small, reversible actions when uncertainty is high.
- Stop if the review is increasing distress without producing a decision-relevant result; return later or consult a trusted person/professional where appropriate.
