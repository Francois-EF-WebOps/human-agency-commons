**Editorial purpose:** Explain how a real solution was identified, selected, implemented, experienced and evaluated. The narrative should be compelling because the evidence and turning points are clear—not because events, quotations or outcomes have been invented.

## 10. Narrative outline

Write the story as sourced scenes or milestones:

1. The pressure
2. The discovery
3. The choice
4. The work and setbacks
5. The first proof
6. The lived result
7. The reality check
8. The next chapter

For each moment, cite the evidence. Do not invent dialogue, motivations, consensus, chronology or causality. If the record does not reveal a moment, describe it as an evidence gap rather than fictionalising it.
