# Contributing

Thank you for helping build a useful, careful, community-oriented commons. Small contributions are welcome; you do not need specialist equipment or a long uninterrupted work session.

## Good first contributions

- Add one relevant source to `evidence/source-register.csv`.
- Add or challenge one specific claim in `evidence/claim-ledger.csv`.
- Document one existing intervention and its limits.
- Translate a public resource or improve plain-language accessibility.
- Identify a missing perspective, likely exclusion, cost, or risk.
- Suggest a small, reversible local test with a measurable outcome.

## Evidence rules

1. Never invent a citation, statistic, quotation, interview, or result.
2. Prefer original documents and credible local sources where available. Record source language and publication date.
3. Preserve the source's meaning; do not quote out of context.
4. Separate what a source says from what a contributor infers.
5. Record uncertainty, contradictory evidence, and important limitations.
6. Do not treat correlation, a single anecdote, popularity, or a persuasive story as proof of effectiveness.
7. Respect copyright, licence conditions, consent, and attribution. Link to material rather than copying it wholesale.

## Intervention proposals

Include the problem, who helped define it, the intended beneficiaries, practical barriers, cost and resource assumptions, evidence, measurable outcomes, possible harms, privacy considerations, how participants can refuse or exit, and stop criteria. Do not present an untested proposal as a proven solution.

## Privacy and safeguarding

Do not publish private contact details, identifying case histories, health information, or sensitive personal data without a clear lawful basis and informed permission. Prefer aggregation and anonymisation. Never expose someone to retaliation to make a contribution more compelling.

## Review process

Open a focused issue or pull request. Keep each contribution small enough to review. Reviewers may request sources, narrower wording, additional perspectives, or removal of sensitive information. Respectful disagreement is part of the project.

## Mobile-friendly contribution

A contribution can be as small as one source URL plus its title, date, language, geographic relevance, and a sentence describing what it supports—and what it does not establish.
