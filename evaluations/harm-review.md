# Harm review checklist

Complete this review before starting an intervention and revisit it during evaluation.

- [ ] Participants understand the purpose, likely burdens, uncertainty, and alternatives.
- [ ] Participation is voluntary; refusal or exit does not trigger punishment.
- [ ] No essential service or entitlement is withheld to compel participation.
- [ ] Costs, time, access requirements, and maintenance are realistic.
- [ ] Privacy risks and sensitive-data collection are minimised.
- [ ] Children and other people needing additional safeguards are protected appropriately.
- [ ] Language, disability, island, income, gender, and other relevant access barriers have been considered without stereotyping.
- [ ] Potential benefits and harms are considered across affected groups.
- [ ] A route exists to report concerns safely.
- [ ] Stop criteria and responsibility for acting on them are explicit.
- [ ] Negative or null results will be recorded, not hidden.
- [ ] No claim of community endorsement is made without actual permission and evidence.

A completed checklist does not guarantee safety. Reassess when circumstances change or participants report unexpected effects.
